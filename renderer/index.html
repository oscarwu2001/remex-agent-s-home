      On screen

      Usage warnings

      A session whose context is nearly full is always flagged: save your work before Claude Code compacts it. Claude Code's own usage-limit messages are passed on too.

      Field feedback (Google Sheet)

      Your field survey’s answers on the board: how many, the average scores and what is new. The app reads one Google Sheet on Monday, Wednesday and Friday at 08:00, and when it starts after missing one: through a small reader script you attach to the sheet, or with your own Google Cloud service account. Only the reader’s secret, or the sheet’s id and a signed sign-in request, are sent, never anything from your sessions. The README says how to set up either.

      Other computers

      See what runs on your other computers under “Running now”. Pick a folder they all sync, such as OneDrive, Dropbox or a network drive, and do the same on each computer. Each one writes a small status file there: project names, statuses, helper types and to-do counts. Never file names, commands, prompts or to-do wording. The app itself never goes online for this; your folder's own sync does.

      Git status folder (pull only): your other computers post a short status every hour, 9:00–19:00, to the agents-status branch, with no Claude tokens. Clone that branch here and pick the clone; the app pulls it every 10 minutes and writes nothing.

      Sessions in the cloud (claude.ai, or the Claude app's cloud tasks) keep no files on your computers, so they can't be shown here. Code-tab sessions and Cowork tasks that run on a computer are shown.

      LINE chats

      Requests for you in your LINE group chats: mentions of your names, your keywords, and what you said you would do. Save a chat from the LINE desktop app into a folder; the app reads only the chats you tick, never changes the files, and goes nowhere online.

      Office team for Claude (optional)

      Performance report

      How each agent has been doing: runs, finished rate, re-runs, time, tokens and a score, by day and by week.

      Demo

      Made-up sessions, helpers, sample field feedback and LINE items, to try the app out. Your own sessions show again when it is off.

      Updates

      Update agents refreshes the office team helpers you added yourself, if a newer version came with the app. It adds nothing new, never turns reminders on, and leaves anything you changed, and all your own agents, skills and hooks, as they are.

      System log

      A record for checking the team works, and for debugging: which agents worked, when, how they finished and their tokens, every few minutes who is working, and any errors. It is kept in the app's own folder, with names and times only, never your prompts or files.
